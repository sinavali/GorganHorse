# Project Proposal — Gorgan Horse Federation Panel

**Document type:** Client-facing proposal
**Prepared for:** Gorgan Horse Federation (هیئت سوارکاری استان گلستان)
**Status:** Final

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The Gorgan Horse Federation currently manages competitions, riders, horses, clubs, signups, and payments through a set of disconnected WordPress plugins, spreadsheets, and manual processes. This has become a bottleneck as the federation's activity grows, with weekly competitions, hundreds of riders, and a growing network of clubs across Golestan province.

This proposal describes a **purpose-built, self-hosted panel** that consolidates all operational work into one place. It is designed to:

- **Replace scattered workflows** with one authoritative system.
- **Eliminate manual data entry** through structured forms and bulk operations.
- **Preserve historical accuracy** through immutable snapshots and audit logs.
- **Give every role exactly what they need** — Admin, Manager, Rider, Club.
- **Remain completely under the federation's control** — no third-party SaaS, no monthly fees, no vendor lock-in.

The panel will be hosted on a **subdomain** of the federation's existing domain (e.g. `panel.gorganhorse.ir`), while the public site (landing pages, blog) continues to run on WordPress on the main domain.

---

## 2. The Problem

Today, the federation faces several challenges:

- **Fragmented tools.** Competitions, riders, horses, and payments live in separate systems that don't talk to each other.
- **Manual reporting.** Reports are generated by hand from spreadsheets, which is slow and error-prone.
- **Payment tracking.** ZarinPal payments are verified manually and reconciled offline.
- **Club coordination.** Clubs have no visibility into their own riders, competitions, and revenue.
- **Rider experience.** Riders have no self-service; every change goes through a Manager.
- **No audit trail.** Changes are untracked, making it hard to understand what happened and when.
- **Scalability ceiling.** The current setup works for a small volume but breaks as the federation grows.

---

## 3. The Solution

A **single, self-hosted web panel** that covers the entire operational lifecycle of the federation:

- **User management** for Admins, Managers, Riders, and Clubs.
- **Club management** with rich profiles, affiliations, and forward-looking bans.
- **Horse management** with ownership, transfers, sharing, galleries, and performance history.
- **Rade management** as reusable competition classes.
- **Payment templates** for centralized pricing and reporting.
- **Competition management** with venue assignment, Rade offerings, capacity limits, and registration windows.
- **Online signup** for riders with integrated ZarinPal payment.
- **Results entry** with draft → confirmed → published workflow.
- **Rich reporting** across all entities with bulk export.
- **Notification center** and broadcast messaging.
- **Full audit trail** of all writes.
- **Backup, restore, reset, and demo seeding** managed from the panel.

The panel is **fully in the federation's hands**: single codebase, single host, no external dependencies, no per-user fees.

## 5. What It Delivers

### 5.1 For Admins

- Complete oversight of users, clubs, horses, competitions, payments, and results.
- Ability to impersonate any user for support.
- Force-logout of all sessions.
- Full settings control.
- Access to audit logs and changelog.
- Backup, restore, reset, and demo tools.

### 5.2 For Managers

- Create and manage competitions, Rades, payments, clubs, riders, horses.
- Confirm or reject signups.
- Enter and publish results.
- Generate rich reports with any filter combination.
- Send broadcast messages to riders.
- Operate without touching settings or backups.

### 5.3 For Riders

- Register and manage their profile.
- Manage their horses, upload images (up to 5 per horse).
- Share horses to other riders (with consent).
- Initiate horse transfers.
- Sign up for competitions with one-click ZarinPal payment.
- View own results, standings, and notifications.

### 5.4 For Clubs

- Rich club profile with logo, banner, address, and contact.
- See affiliated riders and competitions held at their venue.
- Ban riders or horses from selecting the club as affiliation.
- Generate reports scoped to their club.
- Access a report-focused dashboard.

---

## 6. Key Capabilities

### 6.1 Competition Management

- Venue assignment, registration windows, dates, rules.
- Multiple Rades per competition, each with its own price, capacity, and auto-confirm setting.
- Pause/resume registration without closing the window.
- Clone previous competitions to save setup time.

### 6.2 Signup & Payment

- Riders pick a Rade, a horse (own or shared), and an affiliation club.
- Integrated ZarinPal payment in IRT (Toman).
- Automatic confirmation option per Rade.
- Manual confirmation by Managers when needed.
- Price snapshots ensure historical reports never change even if prices are updated.

### 6.3 Horse Ownership

- Every horse tied to exactly one rider.
- Multi-step transfer flow with owner consent.
- Sharing to specific riders.
- Soft delete preserved for historical reporting.
- "Sold to non-rider" status for retired horses.

### 6.4 Results

- Draft → Confirmed → Published flow.
- Barrage flag and notes per Rade.
- Rider notification on publish.
- Only Admin can reopen after publish.

### 6.5 Reports

- One unified reporting page with rich filters.
- Column visibility and order saved per user.
- Bulk export to XLSX and CSV.
- Signed download links valid for one hour.
- Report sharing within the panel (Riders → Managers/Admins; Managers/Admins → anyone).

### 6.6 Notifications & Messaging

- In-panel notification center.
- Broadcast messages to all riders, a competition, a Rade, or selected users.
- Optional SMS delivery via MelyPayamak (disabled by default).

### 6.7 Printing & QR

- Every entity has an A4 print view.
- Competition paper, signup sheet, standings, entity profiles.
- QR codes on printed documents link to the same view in the panel.

### 6.8 SMS & OTP

- Optional SMS integration with MelyPayamak.
- OTP login (5-digit, 2-minute TTL).
- SMS notifications for signup, payment, confirmation, results, transfer, ban.
- All settings managed from the panel.

## 8. Delivery Principles

The panel is built on the following non-negotiable principles:

- **Monolithic deployment.** One folder. Copy-paste to deploy.
- **No third-party SaaS.** The panel runs on the federation's own server.
- **No monthly fees.** No subscriptions, no per-user pricing.
- **No vendor lock-in.** The entire codebase is the federation's asset.
- **Self-contained.** No Composer, no Node, no build step required at runtime.
- **Persian-first.** RTL, Jalali calendar, Persian digits, Toman currency.
- **Documented.** Every file, method, and endpoint is documented.
- **Audited.** Every write is logged with actor, target, and diff.
- **Backup-ready.** One-click backup, restore, and reset.
- **Mobile-first.** Every page works on a phone.
- **Print-ready.** Every entity has an A4 print view.
- **Accessible.** Keyboard navigation and screen reader friendly.

## 9. Integrations

| Integration | Purpose | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **ZarinPal** | Online payment in IRT (Toman) | Required for signups |
| **MelyPayamak** | SMS notifications and OTP | Disabled by default; Admin enables |
| **WordPress (main domain)** | Public site, blog, landing pages | Handled separately, no session sharing |

No other external services are required. The panel is fully self-hosted.

---

## 10. Security & Compliance

- **Encrypted sessions** with HttpOnly, SameSite cookies.
- **CSRF protection** on every state-changing request.
- **Rate limiting** on login, OTP, and sensitive endpoints.
- **Captcha** on login, signup, and OTP request.
- **Prepared SQL statements** everywhere.
- **Upload safety** — MIME sniffing, UUID filenames, no PHP execution.
- **Audit trail** of every write.
- **Password hashing** with PHP's `PASSWORD_DEFAULT`.
- **Data stays in Iran.** No data leaves the federation's server.

---

## 11. Deployment & Ownership

### 11.1 Deployment

- One folder, copied to any PHP 8.1+ host with SQLite support.
- Works out of the box on Apache.
- Nginx configuration is documented.
- No cron, no queue, no background workers.

### 11.2 Ownership

- The federation owns the entire codebase.
- No external services depend on third-party accounts.
- Backup files can be stored anywhere the federation chooses.

### 11.3 Maintenance

- The panel is designed to run for years with minimal intervention.
- Data retention and cleanup are automated.
- Settings are managed from the panel, not from config files.

---

## 12. What Is Not Included

To set clear expectations, the following are **not** part of this project:

- Public site (landing pages, blog, SEO) — handled by WordPress on the main domain.
- WordPress API — developed separately if needed.
- Email sending — not implemented; password reset is handled manually by Managers.
- PWA — not implemented.
- Dark mode — not implemented.
- Multi-tenancy — not implemented.
- Native mobile apps — not implemented.
- Migration from the old WordPress data — starting fresh.

3. **Any report** can be generated and exported in under 30 seconds.
4. **Clubs can view their own performance** without contacting the federation.
5. **Every write is traceable** through the audit trail.

7. **The system runs on a single server** with no external dependencies.

The Gorgan Horse Federation Panel replaces fragmented tools with one coherent, self-hosted, Persian-first operational system. It gives every role exactly what they need, preserves historical accuracy, and keeps the entire operation under the federation's control.

This is not an incremental upgrade. It is the operational backbone for the federation's next decade of growth.
